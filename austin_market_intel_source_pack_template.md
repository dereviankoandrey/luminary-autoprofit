## Selection Rules
Only include items that meet at least one of these:
1. changes underwriting assumptions
2. changes capital availability or deal timing
3. signals real supply/demand movement
4. identifies a new risk or opportunity in a tracked submarket
5. is actionable for operators, investors, or lenders

## Exclusion Rules
Do not include items that are:
- generic real estate news with no Austin operating impact
- opinion with no underlying evidence
- duplicate summaries of the same event
- too weak to change a decision or watchlist view

## End-of-Issue QA
Before drafting the issue, confirm:
- at least 5 high-signal items captured
- at least 3 submarkets supported by evidence
- at least 1 capital-markets or transactions signal
- at least 1 supply or permit signal
- every claim has a source line
