## Concrete signals already visible from the draft agenda
1. **Density-bonus / incentive-program changes (Item 35)**  
   The draft agenda shows an ordinance amending density-bonus and incentive-program requirements, including allowing some development applications to be processed before the land-use restriction agreement is signed, allowing fee waivers, and requiring an on-site professional relocation specialist.

2. **Dog's Head mega-site framework (Items 37-38)**  
   The draft agenda shows a proposed **45-year development agreement** and related interlocal structure covering roughly **2,614 acres** in the area generally known as **Dog's Head**, with implications for allowable land uses, site-development rules, annexation conditions, temporary right-of-way fees, income-restricted housing, and TIRZ / local-government-corporation mechanics.

3. **Barton Springs Zone redevelopment exception (Item 45)**  
   The draft agenda shows a redevelopment-exception item tied to the proposed **SoLa Mixed Use** project on South Lamar, including a stated Barton Springs Zone mitigation fee.

4. **East Riverside corridor movement (Items 56 and 63)**  
   The draft agenda shows both an **ERC Regulating Plan amendment** for **1404 East Riverside Drive** and a separate **1404 East Riverside PUD** hearing, reinforcing that corridor-level entitlement movement is still active and relevant.
