## Executive Recommendation

The best first wedge is not "AI agency for everyone." It is a narrow local-service operator offer:

> Recover missed demand, improve reviews, and reactivate old leads for multi-location local service businesses.

Prioritize sectors where:

- each missed call or bad review has visible revenue impact
- demand is urgent or appointment-based
- franchise/multi-location operators already buy standardized local marketing systems
- owners are easy to identify
- reputation and speed-to-lead are measurable

The strongest starting cluster is home services + cleaning/restoration + automotive service. A second cluster is health/wellness and senior/pet care, but those need more compliance and trust handling.

### Technical Plan

Do not start with broad scraping. Start with a controlled scout:

1. Pick one geography and one vertical.
2. Build a seed list of 100-200 businesses.
3. For each business, capture:
   - name
   - location
   - brand/franchise
   - website
   - phone
   - Google rating/review count
   - last 10 review sentiment tags
   - owner/operator if public
   - LinkedIn URL if found
   - booking/contact form URL
   - estimated lead-flow issue
4. Score using a simple opportunity model.
5. Generate a short personalized audit.
6. Human approves outbound.

Suggested score:

```text
Opportunity = high_ticket + urgency + review_gap + slow_response + multi_location + contactability - compliance_risk - corporate_control
```

### Automation Boundaries

- AI can gather and summarize public data.
- AI can draft audits and outreach.
- A human approves outbound messaging.
- No auto-posting LinkedIn.
- No cold-email sending until domain/reputation infrastructure and opt-out handling are configured.

## Competitive Landscape

### Agency Platforms / Local Marketing

- Scorpion: strong in home services and law firms; AI intake/conversion/reputation positioning validates the pain but competes at a more mature platform level.
- Broad local SEO/reputation tools: Birdeye, Podium, Reputation.com, NiceJob, Broadly, Grade.us and similar tools handle reviews/messages but usually do not deliver a full vertical AI operator.
- Franchise/local marketing agencies: many sell SEO/PPC/reputation, but fewer package missed-call recovery + review recovery + reactivation as an AI-led operator.

### AI Agency Competitors

The "AI agency" market is crowded and noisy. Most competitors sell generic chatbots, automations, or cold-email systems. The defense is a narrow vertical offer with measurable outcomes:

- missed leads recovered
- booked calls
- review volume/rating lift
- reactivated old customers
- speed-to-lead reduction
