# Austin Entitlement Signal Brief — Narrowed Offer Spec

## Purpose
Re-scope the broader Austin Market Intel concept into a tighter recurring product with stronger information asymmetry, lower bespoke risk, and higher agent autonomy.

## Why Narrow It
The current sample issue proved that the most reliable public signals are not generic transaction headlines. They are entitlement, permit, code, board, and process changes that affect underwriting confidence and project timelines.

That suggests a better wedge:
- fewer weak macro/news items
- more operator-useful local signals
- easier repeatable source collection
- stronger differentiation than a generic Austin market newsletter

## Core Offer
A weekly Austin development-signal brief for developers, lenders, acquisition teams, and consultants focused on:
- permit/process changes
- entitlement and board/council signals
- code or review-path updates
- public pipeline / affordable-housing / delivery signals
- concise operator implications for underwriting and deal triage

## Positioning
Not a broad market newsletter.

Position as:
**the Austin entitlement-and-permit signal layer for people underwriting local development risk.**

## Best-Fit Buyer
- small and midsize developers
- lenders and debt brokers screening local projects
- acquisition teams evaluating timeline/process risk
- consultants who need a compact local signal digest

## Candidate Deliverable Structure
1. Executive summary
2. Top 5-8 new signals
3. Process / code / board watchlist
4. Underwriting implications
5. Near-term operator triggers
6. Source log

## Strongest Repeatable Source Stack Right Now
- Austin Development Services pages
- Austin boards and commissions pages
- Austin council pages
- Austin housing / AHOST pages
- BLS Austin macro page as light context only
- Austin Open Data / dataset-specific links once stable endpoints are identified

## What To Exclude
- generic national macro commentary
- thinly sourced transaction gossip
- bespoke property-by-property research
- broad “everything in Austin” coverage

## Pricing Hypothesis
- solo tier: $99/month
- team tier: $299/month
- premium custom watchlist only after the base issue proves repeatable

## Why This Is Better Than The Broad Version
- higher information asymmetry
- lower research sprawl
- stronger workflow fit with current reachable sources
- more defensible than generic local market commentary
- easier for an agent to produce with light human QA

## Main Risks
- still needs sharper submarket/project linkage over time
- public source extraction may stay noisy on some city pages
- buyer pool is narrower than a broad market-intel product

## Pass Standard
This wedge passes if one issue can be assembled in under 75 minutes with:
- 5-8 source-backed local signals
- at least 3 directly tied to entitlement/process/permit risk
- light human QA only
- a clear reason a local operator would pay for the brief

## Smallest Next Test
Produce one sample issue explicitly using this narrower positioning and judge whether it feels more decision-useful than the broader Austin market-intel framing.
