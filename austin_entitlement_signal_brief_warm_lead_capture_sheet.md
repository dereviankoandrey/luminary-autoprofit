# Austin Entitlement Signal Brief — Warm Lead Capture Sheet

**Status:** execution asset  
**Created:** 2026-05-13 05:00 UTC cycle

## Purpose
Make it easy to brain-dump 15-20 warm Austin-relevant names before scoring the first 10.

Use this before:
- `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_first10_selection_sheet.md`
- `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_outreach_tracker.md`

## Why this exists
The current blocker is not packaging.

The blocker is getting real names out of Andrey's network fast enough to start pricing tests.

This sheet is designed to reduce blank-page friction.

## 15-minute capture rule
Do this quickly.

1. Set a 15-minute timer.
2. List names without scoring yet.
3. Favor warm and relevant over prestigious.
4. Stop once 15-20 names are listed.
5. Then move the best names into the selection sheet.

## Best buckets to check
### Bucket 1 — Developers and acquisitions
Think of:
- Austin-area developers
- multifamily or mixed-use operators
- acquisitions people screening land or infill deals
- owner-operators who care about timelines and local process risk

### Bucket 2 — Lenders and debt brokers
Think of:
- people screening local development loans
- debt brokers who see weak deals early
- lenders who care about entitlement and permit timing risk

### Bucket 3 — Land-use and development consultants
Think of:
- zoning / entitlement consultants
- civil / planning contacts with developer clients
- owner-rep or project-management groups exposed to city-process friction

### Bucket 4 — Capital and adjacent operators
Think of:
- capital partners with Austin exposure
- architects or engineers who may want a client-facing signal digest
- people adjacent to development workflows who still feel timing risk

## Fast memory prompts
Use any prompt that helps names surface.

- Who has complained about Austin process friction before?
- Who regularly asks what city or council changes mean?
- Who screens Austin deals often enough to care about missed signals?
- Who could say yes to a low-ticket paid pilot without committee friction?
- Who is warm enough that a short message would feel natural?
- Who would give a blunt useful answer even if they do not buy?
