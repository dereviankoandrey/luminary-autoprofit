# AI Underwriting SaaS — Beta Target List

**Last updated:** 2026-04-15 10:00 UTC  
**Purpose:** convert the underwriting MVP and beta packet into an actionable outreach list once external messaging is appropriate.

## Goal
Identify 3-5 high-fit beta testers who can validate whether the underwriting tool saves time, improves screening consistency, and is worth paying for.

## Ideal First Testers
Prioritize contacts who match all or most of these:
- screen deals regularly
- feel pain around first-pass triage or analyst time
- can give fast practical feedback
- are relationship-close enough to say yes to a low-friction beta
- do not require enterprise procurement or long sales cycles

## Priority Segments

### 1. Small Multifamily Operators
**Why first:** close fit with existing underwriting logic and recurring deal screening.

**Best profile**
- screens 5+ deals per month
- values quick GO / REVIEW / NO-GO triage
- may want team access later

**What to ask**
- How many deals do you screen monthly?
- What gets rejected fastest today?
- Would a first-pass screen save analyst time?

## 2. Private Lenders / Debt Brokers
**Why second:** fast pain point around early disqualification and consistency.

**Best profile**
- sees frequent inbound opportunities
- needs quick initial filter before deeper diligence
- values explainable outputs and red-flag surfacing

**What to ask**
- How many inbound opportunities do you reject early?
- What are your main instant disqualifiers?
- Would a consistent first-pass screen help your process?

## 3. Small GP / Syndicator Teams
**Why third:** may have enough deal flow to become paid recurring users.

**Best profile**
- founder-led or lean acquisitions team
- inconsistent analyst bandwidth
- active in value-add or opportunistic acquisitions

**What to ask**
- How often do weak deals consume unnecessary time?
- Would you use this weekly if outputs stayed reliable?
- Would team access matter?
