# Moonshot Roadmap

**Mission:** Help and empower 1 billion people to use agentic AI to grow and create abundance.
**Owner:** Andrey Derevianko
**First Step:** OpenClaw/AICSAI system (current infrastructure)

---

## Pillar 1: Real Estate Exit / Time Freedom

**Objective:** Sell or partially exit real estate holdings to free capital and reclaim time.

### Phase 1 — Research & Prep (Weeks 1-4)
- [ ] Inventory all properties: value, income, equity, carrying costs
- [ ] Research current market conditions in each market
- [ ] Get professional appraisals or broker price opinions
- [ ] Identify optimal exit strategy per property (sell, refi + hold, 1031 exchange, partial)
- [ ] Estimate after-tax proceeds and capital deployment options

### Phase 2 — Execution (Weeks 5-12)
- [ ] List priority property first
- [ ] Prepare marketing materials (photos, descriptions, financials)
- [ ] Engage buyer agents or list directly
- [ ] Negotiate and close
- [ ] Reallocate freed capital

**Primary Agent:** Luminary (business execution), CFO Company (financial analysis)

## Pillar 2: TriTrack Global Implementation

**Objective:** Make TriTrack a real, globally available product.

### Phase 1 — Product Foundation (Weeks 1-4)
- [ ] Document current TriTrack state: features, tech stack, gaps
- [ ] Define minimum viable global product (what does "global" mean functionally?)
- [ ] Identify technical blockers vs. business blockers
- [ ] Competitive analysis: who owns this space globally?

### Phase 2 — Build & Validate (Weeks 5-12)
- [ ] Prioritize and build core features
- [ ] Beta testing with real users
- [ ] Feedback loop and iteration
- [ ] Infrastructure for multi-region deployment

### Phase 3 — Go-to-Market (Weeks 13-24)
- [ ] Pricing strategy
- [ ] Channel partnerships
- [ ] Marketing and launch
- [ ] Scale and optimize

**Primary Agent:** Luminary (product/business direction), Apex (strategy), CRO (growth)
