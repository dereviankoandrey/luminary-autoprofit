## Commentary: What This Means Right Now

### 1. Summer Gap = Strategic Preparation Window

The five-week council recess means **no new entitlement decisions** at the full Council level until July 9. For developers with active projects in Austin, this period offers a strategic advantage:

- **No new political pressure** or last-minute agenda items to complicate pending applications
- Staff review times continue normally (committee meetings still process routine items)
- Applications submitted now will be queued for immediate processing after the July 9 reconvening

### 2. Commercial Plan Review Process — Stable and Documented

The Commercial Building Plan Review Division operates on a well-established framework:

- **Preliminary Plan Review (PPR)** available through Expedited Building Program — hourly consultation to identify code issues before formal submission
- **Quick Turnaround (QT):** Same-day approval for eligible small finish-outs and interior remodels
- **7 Business Day Review:** Small commercial remodels in designated occupancy types
- **Standard New Construction:** 25 business days initial, 15 for update cycles

The **2024 Technical Codes** took effect July 2025. All applications submitted after July 10 must comply with these codes — this is well-documented and not a moving target.

### 3. Four-Unit Development Path Clarified (October 2025 Update)

Four-unit developments are now explicitly exempt from the site plan process and classified as multi-family residential. This simplifies the approval path for small multifamily projects:

- Can use **Residential Plan Review** or **Residential Expedited Plan Review**
- Single building permit application covers all four units
- Not applicable to four separate single-family homes, triplex + detached, duplex combinations, or townhouse configurations (these remain Residential Plan Review)
- Fourplex definition: single building, four distinct units with independent entrance/kitchen/bathroom
