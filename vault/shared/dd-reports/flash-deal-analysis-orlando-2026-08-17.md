- **Property tax rate:** ~1.15% (Florida — no state income tax advantage)
- **Insurance drag:** $3,600–$6,000/yr (mandatory windstorm + homeowners; Florida is highest-drag state in pipeline)
- **Employment anchors:** Tourism (Disney/Walt Disney World, Universal), healthcare (AdventHealth, Orlando Health), aerospace (Air Force base proximity), growing tech sector (Amazon fulfillment network, Salesforce presence)
- **Net migration:** ~+40K/year (still positive but decelerating from pandemic peaks of +80K/year)

**Key Orlando differentiator:** Tourism-dependent economy creates unique rental demand patterns — short-term rental competition is intense in tourist corridors but traditional long-term rentals remain strong near employment hubs. Insurance costs are the single largest drag variable across ALL Florida markets tested.
