### Section C: Financial Structure Analysis

The deal uses an unconventional financing structure:

1. **Bond Defeasance:** $5M GO bonds from 2025 series being defeased (replaced)
   - Escrow agreement with U.S. Bank Trust Company
   - Upfront payment comes from Greystar (private developer partner)
   - This means the city gets cash today, Greystar gets relief on future debt service obligations

2. **Ground Lease Structure:**
   - City retains land ownership
   - Developer builds and operates
   - Revenue split governed by MDA terms
   - HA/COA administers affordable housing components

3. **What We Can't Price Without More Data:**
   - Ground lease term length (15? 30? 50 years?)
   - Base rent vs. percentage rent structure
   - Affordable unit count and income limits
   - Development phasing schedule
   - Financing costs for Greystar's portion

**Assessment:** Without the MDA terms, any pro forma is speculation. The deal is fundamentally an institutional-level transaction between a city government and a national operator — not a traditional investment opportunity for individual operators.
