# Underwriting SaaS — 10 Warm Contact Targets

## Context
- Product: AI Underwriting engine (Day 9+ deploy-ready)
- Pipeline: Deal Analysis Suite Bundle includes this as Lane 1
- Goal: Get deployed → get first customers → validate pricing ($497/report or $997/mo subscription)
- These are archetypal contacts based on what we know about Andrey's real estate network in TX/GA/FL

---

## Target 1: Atlanta-area multifamily syndicator (50+ units)
**Archetype:** Active deal maker, needs faster underwriting to compete
**Why them:** Sells deals faster = wins more deals. If their competitor underwrites in minutes vs hours, they buy.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], I've been building an AI underwriting engine that runs full 6-metric pro formas (IRR, cash-on-cash, debt-service-coverage, LTV, DCR, spread) on multifamily and commercial deals in under a minute — same analysis that takes your team 2-4 hours. Want me to run one of your current pipeline deals through it so you can see the output? Free for you to try."

## Target 2: GA regional credit union or community bank lending officer
**Archetype:** Makes small commercial loans ($1M-$5M), needs underwriting speed and documentation
**Why them:** Banks are pressured to move faster too. Automated underwriting = fewer man-hours, more deals processed.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], we built an AI engine that auto-generates full underwriting packages for commercial real estate loans — financial analysis, risk assessment, scenario modeling. It's designed as a force multiplier for smaller lending teams. If you're still doing pro forma work in spreadsheets, this could save your team 60-80% of the time per deal. Worth a 10-minute demo?"

## Target 3: Florida multifamily broker/wholesaler
**Archetype:** Sources deals for out-of-state investors, needs instant analysis to close fast
**Why them:** Speed is their entire business model. Instant underwriting = faster offers = more wins.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], quick question — when you find a great deal and need to underwrite it overnight so you can make a competitive offer by morning, how long does that take you right now? We just built an engine that spits out full underwriting packages in ~60 seconds. If you want to test it on one of your active deals, I'll run it free for you."

## Target 4: National REIA chapter leader or local investor group president
**Archetype:** Influences dozens/hundreds of investors in a market
**Why them:** One champion → network effect. If they adopt, everyone adopts.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], I'm rolling out an AI underwriting tool for real estate investors and looking for a few chapter leaders to test it before we go public. It takes any deal in (address or inputs), runs full underwriting across all key metrics, and produces an investor-ready report with risk assessment. Would your members find this useful? If so, I'll set up a free group demo."

## Target 5: Real estate fund manager / small fund operator ($5M-$50M AUM)
**Archetype:** Manages portfolio of deals, needs consistent underwriting standards
**Why them:** Fund operators need audit trails and standardized analysis across their pipeline. AI provides consistency humans can't scale to.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], quick one — your fund is probably processing 10-20 deals per quarter through underwriting. We built an AI engine that standardizes all the analysis: same methodology, every time, no human inconsistency. You get full scenario modeling, risk tiering, and investor-ready reports automatically. Curious if that's a problem you've thought about solving."

## Target 6: Property management company with acquisition arm
**Archetype:** Owns/operates properties but also evaluates deals to buy
**Why them:** Already has market data from their operations — combining operational intel with AI underwriting = unfair advantage.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], property managers have an edge that pure investors don't — you see the actual numbers (vacancy, repairs, rent rolls) before any deal closes. We built an engine that combines that kind of operational intelligence with full automated underwriting so your acquisition decisions are backed by both real data and rigorous financial analysis. Want to try it?"

## Target 7: Real estate attorney handling transaction work
**Archetype:** Reviews deals, knows where investors mess up underwriting
**Why them:** They see the mistakes — over-leveraged deals, poor DSCR assumptions. They'd want their clients to use proper tools.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], you've seen enough investor deals to know how many get the numbers wrong on their own. We built an AI underwriting engine that catches the stuff most amateur/intermediate investors miss: unrealistic rent growth, understated vacancy, DSCR below lender minimums, scenario stress tests. Worth a quick demo?"

## Target 8: Real estate coaching program / course creator
**Archetype:** Sells education to hundreds of investors, needs tools to offer as value-add
**Why them:** They're always looking for proprietary tools they can bundle with their programs. This gives them differentiation.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], if you ever thought about offering your students a deal analysis tool instead of just teaching them how to use spreadsheets — we built one that runs full AI underwriting in seconds. You could white-label it as part of your program. Your students get actual results; you get a unique value proposition no one else has. Open to exploring this?"

## Target 9: Commercial real estate (CRE) broker
**Archetype:** Brokers office, industrial, or retail deals — needs underwriting for tenant-buyer analysis
**Why them:** CRE deals are even more complex than multifamily. Faster underwriting = faster client service = more commissions.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], when a buyer comes to you with an LOI on a commercial property, how long does it take to run the actual numbers they'd need? We built an engine that does full CRE underwriting in ~60 seconds — cap rates, NOI analysis, sensitivity tables, financing scenarios. Could save you 2-3 hours per deal and keep your buyers in the game longer."

## Target 10: Angel investor / family office principal focused on proptech
**Archetype:** Invests in or advises real estate tech companies
**Why them:** Not a customer — a connector AND potential investor. If they believe in the product, they open doors to their entire network.
**Message draft:**
> "Hey [Name], I'm building an AI underwriting engine for commercial and multifamily real estate and curious what you think. The thesis: every real estate deal needs underwriting, but it's still done in spreadsheets by people who've never seen a proper model — or takes 4 hours if they do it right. We automated the whole thing. Would love your take on the product and whether there's anyone in your network where this would be a game-changer."

## Execution Notes
- **Personalize:** Replace bracketed placeholders, adjust tone to match relationship (formal vs casual)
- **Warm intro preferred:** These work best sent through existing connections or mutuals
- **First message goal is always demo/trial, not sale:** Let the product sell itself
- **Follow-up cadence:** 3 touches over 14 days if no response; then move to nurture

- Verify each target's current deal activity and relevance
- Find recent deals they've done (public records, press) for personalized hooks
- Research their exact title and preferred communication channel
