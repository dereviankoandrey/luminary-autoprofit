## Market Positioning Note

Indianapolis represents the **cash-flow corridor** of the validated market portfolio. Unlike Sun Belt markets (Dallas, Nashville, Charlotte) where appreciation drives returns, Indianapolis is a pure cash-on-cash play at 5-7% yields with low volatility. This makes it attractive for conservative investors and institutional co-investment partners seeking stable, inflation-hedged income streams.
