## Market Context (July 2026)

San Antonio continues to attract migration-driven demand from both domestic relocation seekers and international buyers, particularly from Latin America. The North Side/Stone Oak corridor commands premium pricing relative to SA metro averages due to top-rated Alamo Heights ISD assignments, golf course communities, and proximity to Joint Base San Antonio facilities.

**Key Market Indicators:**
- Median single-family price: ~$385K (vs Austin $490K, Dallas $420K)
- Price per unit for multifamily: ~$105K–$125K/unit (vs Austin $160K+ / Dallas $140K+)
- Cap rate spread: 3.8%–5.2% for stabilized product
- Vacancy: ~6.2% for multifamily (slightly above national avg)
- Rental growth: 3.5% YoY, lagging Austin's 5%+ but more sustainable
- Military demand base: JBSA provides floor under multifamily vacancy
- Development pipeline: Moderate — fewer large institutional projects than DA or DFW

**Positioning:** San Antonio offers the best entry-point pricing among major Texas markets. Investors seeking cash-on-cash yield over upside speculation will find it most compelling. The market is less volatile than Austin but has lower appreciation potential — a different risk/return profile that our engine handles deterministically.

- Gross profit: $65K on $210K total = **30.9% margin before financing costs**

### Key due diligence items:
1. Foundation inspection (SA clay soil — non-negotiable for 30+ year old bungalows)
2. Permit history search at City of SA Development Services
3. Comparable sold validation within 800 feet, sold within 90 days
4. Contractor bid verification (not estimator guesswork)
