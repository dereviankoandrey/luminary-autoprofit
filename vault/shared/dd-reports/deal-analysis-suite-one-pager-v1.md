## Three Lanes, One System

### 1. Instant Underwriting
Multifamily, fix-and-flip, development — run any deal through the engine with your custom parameters. Output includes:
- GO / NO-GO / REVIEW recommendation with confidence score
- Full scenario analysis (base, adverse, optimistic)
- Key metrics: cap rate, DSCR, CoC return, IRR, equity multiple
- Risk flags tied to specific line items

### 2. Due Diligence Reports
For deals that pass the underwriting screen — get a full DD package including market context, comparable analysis, verification checklists, and documented data sources. Format: $197–$497/report post-beta. Current sample: $0.

### 3. Austin Permit Velocity Monitor *(active cycles only)*
Ongoing tracking of Austin building department activity — permits issued, plan review timelines, inspection pass rates — surfaced as weekly briefs. Early signal on market shifts most developers miss until the data is stale.
