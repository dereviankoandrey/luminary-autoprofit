## Usage Protocol

1. **Andrey sends from his personal email** with his signature (name, title, contact info). Not company branding yet — personal credibility is the differentiator.
2. **Add 1 specific personalization detail** before each send — their recent deal, LinkedIn activity, fund focus. Takes ~2 minutes per person and makes a massive difference in open/reply rates.
3. **Do NOT mention "AI" or "autonomous"** in subject lines or first email body. Lead with operator credibility and workflow value. The mechanism (how it works) is secondary to the outcome.
4. **Include sample report link** when available — concrete proof beats abstract claims every time.
5. **Track responses** using the Outreach Execution Tracker (`outreach-execution-tracker-v1.md`) — note who engages, what questions they ask, and which segments show strongest interest.
6. **Send 3-5 first emails per day**, one week apart minimum between segments. Consistency > volume in this approach because every message is semi-personalized.
