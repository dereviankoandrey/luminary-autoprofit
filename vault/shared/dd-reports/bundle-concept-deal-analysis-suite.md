## The Pitch

**"Your entire deal analysis workflow, automated.** Underwrite deals in seconds, get institutional-grade due diligence reports, and access live Austin market intelligence — all from one subscription."

### Why bundle?
- Investors evaluate deals as **processes**, not isolated questions. They need underwriting → DD report → market context in sequence.
- Pitching three separate products creates decision fatigue ("which tool do I need?"). A suite eliminates that friction.
- Bundle pricing unlocks significantly higher AR ($997–$2,497 initial package vs $197 per report or $297/mo per product).
- Aligns with the agentic AI thesis: end-to-end workflows over disconnected point tools.

## The Three Tiers

### Tier 1: Starter — $497 setup + $97/mo
**For:** Individual investors evaluating occasional deals
- **Underwriting SaaS access:** Unlimited deal screenings (5 per session)
- **DD Reports:** 2 reports included in setup, then $197/report after
- **Permit Velocity Monitor:** Quarterly market digest during active cycles
- Delivery: self-serve + monthly check-in

### Tier 2: Pro — $997 setup + $197/mo
**For:** Active investors running 2–5 deals per month
- **Underwriting SaaS access:** Unlimited deal screenings (unlimited sessions)
- **DD Reports:** 5 reports included in setup, then $147/report after (30% discount)
- **Permit Velocity Monitor:** Monthly briefs during active cycles
- Delivery: priority processing + monthly strategy call

### Tier 3: Partner — $2,497 setup + $497/mo
**For:** Small teams and developers evaluating multiple deals simultaneously
- **Underwriting SaaS access:** Unlimited deal screenings (unlimited sessions), custom metrics dashboards
- **DD Reports:** 15 reports included in setup, then $97/report after (50% discount)
- **Permit Velocity Monitor:** Monthly briefs + quarterly deep-dive market analysis
- Delivery: white-glove onboarding + bi-weekly strategy calls + direct agent channel

## Key Positioning Notes

1. **Don't lead with "AI"** — lead with outcomes ("automated deal analysis," "institutional-grade DD reports"). Investors care about better decisions, not the tech stack.
2. **Position as an analyst substitute** — not a tool. Analysts cost $10K-$50K per engagement and take 2-6 weeks. This costs $497 and delivers in hours.
3. **Highlight speed + rigor combo** — most alternatives are either fast/shallow (online calculators) or slow/rigorous (advisory firms). No one offers both.
4. **Beta framing reduces friction** — early pricing, feedback-oriented language ("help me improve this"), limited capacity ("looking for 3-5 operators").

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Bundle perceived as overpriced | Offer individual product pricing as fallback; emphasize included value (15 DD reports = $2,955 standalone value) |
| "AI" skepticism from conservative investors | Lead with analyst-quality framing; include confidence scores and transparent math they can verify |
| Scope creep — too many features confusing buyers | Keep messaging tight: underwriting → DD report → market intel. One page, three bullets |
| Liability exposure on GO/NO-GO recommendations | Strong disclaimer everywhere; position as "analytical framework, not financial advice" |
