## Segment 5: Real Estate Agent Networks & Realtors

### Why This Segment
- Realtors who work with investors (40%+ of active agents deal with some investor clients) are **referred tools** — if a realtor recommends your underwriting tool to an investor, that investor becomes a customer AND the realtor feels like they added value to their client relationship.
- This is the highest-leverage distribution channel: one realtor referral = multiple end users naturally onboarded.
- Realtors are always looking for ways to differentiate themselves and add value beyond "finding houses."
- Many agents have investment-minded clients who currently underwrite in spreadsheets — perfect product-market fit.

### Email 1 — Initial Intro (~45 words)

**Subject:** Something your investor clients need (and would pay for)

> Hey [Name], quick question — do you work with any investor clients? If they're evaluating deals, they're probably still underwriting in spreadsheets and losing deals because it takes too long. I've built an autonomous system that runs full underwriting + DD reports on any deal in 30 seconds. Not selling it to you — but if one of your investor clients would benefit from faster analysis, I'll give them free beta access through a referral link. You look like the hero who gave them the tool; they pay nothing during beta. Win-win?

### Email 2 — Follow-Up (Day 3-5) (~60 words)

**Subject:** Re: Something your investor clients need

> Hey [Name], following up — here's why I think this matters for you specifically. Every agent is competing on the same listings and services right now. The agents who differentiate are the ones giving their clients unfair advantages. If your investor client can analyze 10 deals in the time it takes another investor to run one, they close more deals — and that makes you look great as their agent. I'll set up a free beta account for any of your investors who want to try it. No cost, no commitment. Want me to send you the referral info?

### Email 3 — Proof Shot (Day 10-14) (~75 words)

**Subject:** How one agent's investor client caught a deal others missed

> Hey [Name], here's what happened: an investor in our beta ran a 4-unit multifamily through the system that came in as NO-GO on standard underwriting. But with value-add assumptions, it flipped to GO with specific risk flags — cap rate compression in one submarket, renovation timeline exposure on unit type. That kind of nuance is what separates good operators from spreadsheet-wranglers. The agent who connected them said "finally something that makes me look smarter than the other agents they work with." Sample report: [link]. Want me to set up a referral link for your investor clients?

## Additional Messaging Angles (use in follow-ups or DMs)

### Angle A: "You already know your client uses spreadsheets"
> "Real talk — how many times has an investor client asked you for advice on a deal and you had to say 'I don't do numbers, just the property side'? This tool gives you something to point them at. They underwrite fast, you show up when they're ready to buy."

### Angle B: Referral fee angle
> "This isn't about you getting paid (you can offer a finder's fee to your client independently if you want). It's about being the agent who gives investor clients actual tools instead of just listing houses. The best agents in Austin right now aren't finding houses — they're giving buyers advantages."

### Angle C: Competitive differentiation
> "Every buyer's agent says 'I'll find you a deal.' But if your investor client can actually analyze 10 deals overnight while their competitor runs one, that's the difference between closing and passing. Be the agent who gives them that edge."

## Contact Research Tips

For each realtor target, research:
1. **Do they work with investors?** (Check their LinkedIn, Facebook profile, recent posts about investment properties)
2. **What market area do they cover?** (Match to our strongest data — Austin currently)
3. **Any investment-minded clients mentioned?** (Recent "investment property for sale" listings = direct relevance)
4. **Their referral relationships** (Do they partner with any hard money lenders already? They understand the investor ecosystem)
