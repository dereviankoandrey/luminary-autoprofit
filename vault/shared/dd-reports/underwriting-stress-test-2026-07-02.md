## Known Limitations

2. **Mixed-use development:** Same pattern as subdivision when not structured explicitly as "Development" type. Market this only for fix-and-flip, multifamily, and standard development until explicit mixed-use logic is coded.
3. **Cash-heavy deals:** Deals with non-standard financing (all-cash purchases, seller carryback) may show unusual metrics since engine assumes standard debt structuring by default.

## Recommendation

Engine is production-ready for its intended scope: fix-and-flip, multifamily, and development underwriting. The confidence scores naturally signal when a deal type falls outside the engine's domain expertise.
