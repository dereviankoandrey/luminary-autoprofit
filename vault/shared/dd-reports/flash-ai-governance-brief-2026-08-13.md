# AI Governance Intelligence Brief — Founders & Operators Edition

## Executive Summary

**What:** Weekly intelligence brief on AI governance developments that directly impact operators building with AI agents. Not compliance theory — operational signals: which frameworks are being adopted, what regulatory deadlines matter for your stack, and how competitors are positioning around trust/governance.

**Why Now:** Lovable raised $400M Series C at $13.3B valuation specifically highlighting governance features (AIUC-1 certification, trust centers, publishing controls). Etherion published comprehensive AI Governance operationalization guide. These aren't compliance stories — they're market signals that buyers are demanding governance as a product feature.

**Format:** 2-page brief delivered weekly via email. First page: top 3 developments this week with operator impact assessment. Second page: one deep-dive on a framework, regulation, or standard relevant to your stack.
