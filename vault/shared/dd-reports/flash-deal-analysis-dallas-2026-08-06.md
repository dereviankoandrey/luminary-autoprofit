**Analysis:** For a pure investor buying the duplex and renting both units: negative cash flow at current interest rates. The 2.8% property tax rate is the primary driver — it adds ~$9,520/year in carrying costs that cannot be offset by rental income alone.

However, for an owner-occupant (house-hack scenario), this converts to a significantly positive economic position:
- Owner saves $1,400/month in imputed rent while paying down equity
- Net cash flow deficit of -$690 is partially offset by the housing benefit
- Total net monthly benefit: approximately **+$710/month** when including imputed rent

**Why Dallas specifically:** Texas's no-state-income-tax status makes the house-hack scenario more attractive than comparable deals in California or New York, where state income tax would further compress after-tax returns. The $25K-$30K annual state tax savings in CA/NY essentially subsidizes the negative cash flow — making the house-hack math work better in Texas even with higher property taxes.

**Bottom line:** Viable as a path to homeownership for owner-occupants willing to accept modest monthly deficits. NOT recommended for pure investors at current rate environment. Consider waiting for rate drops below 6% before pursuing this deal type as a cash-flow play.
