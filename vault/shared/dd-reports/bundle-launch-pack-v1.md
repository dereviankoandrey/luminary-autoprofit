**What you're selling:** A single subscription that replaces three separate tools investors currently buy or do manually:
1. Deal underwriting (spreadsheets, $497/report standalone)
2. Due diligence reports ($297-497/report standalone)  
3. Austin permitting intelligence ($497/month during active cycles)

**Why bundle works:** Investors buy outcomes, not tools. "We analyze deals before you write checks" is stronger than "we sell X, Y, and Z separately." Bundle pricing: $197/mo (Underwriting), $297/mo (DD Reports), $497/mo (Permit + Underwriting).

**Key differentiation against competitors:**
- 30-second analysis vs. 2-4 hours of manual work
- Scenario stress testing (most competitors show only base case)
- Public data integration, not fabricated numbers — flags when data is insufficient rather than guessing
- GO/NO-GO/REVIEW recommendations with confidence scores

## Key Rules for Sending

1. **DO NOT mention "AI" in subject lines** — investors respond to workflow value first, mechanism second
2. **Send from personal email** — this is a beta invitation from you, not a company blast
3. **First message goal is always demo/trial, not sale** — let the product sell itself
4. **Include at least one specific detail about their business** in every email (proves you're not spamming)
5. **Track responses manually in a simple table** — who responded, what they asked, next action
