## Market Profile

Atlanta is the first Southeast mega-metro tested — a different scale and economic engine from the Sun Belt mid-tier cities already analyzed (Tampa, Miami, Dallas). Key structural characteristics:

- **Economic diversity:** Fortune 500 HQs (Coca-Cola, Home Depot, UPS, Delta) provide employment stability not seen in single-industry markets
- **Migration tailwind:** ~40K net domestic migrants/year — strong but diluted by larger population base
- **Insurance profile:** Moderate hurricane/flood risk in coastal counties; inland metro has lower exposure than FL/GA coast. Premiums rising but 15-20% below Florida averages.
- **Tax environment:** Georgia state income tax (4.75% top bracket) vs no state income tax in TX/FL — a meaningful carry-cost differential for investors from high-tax states
- **Inventory dynamics:** 3.8 months of supply at current sales pace — seller's market, down from 5.2 months in Q1 2026 but still constrained

**Positioning relative to pipeline:** Atlanta sits between Dallas (large TX metro with no state income tax) and Tampa (FL Sun Belt migration play). It offers the best of both: large-metro economic depth without Texas-level carry cost advantage, or Florida-level migration intensity.
