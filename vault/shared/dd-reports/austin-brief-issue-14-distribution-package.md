## Target Persona Matrix

### Segment A: Active Austin Developers / Operators
**Profile:** Currently underwriting deals in Austin or adjacent markets; they have deal flow now, not "someday." They care about entitlement timelines, permitting velocity, capital costs — the things that kill pro formas.
- **Where to find them:** Local REIA meetings, LinkedIn (search: "Austin real estate developer"), A360 community members, past conference contacts (URBANland, CREW)
- **Message angle:** Time-to-permitting is the single hidden cost variable in Austin underwriting right now

### Segment B: Real Estate Investors in Sun Belt / Growth Markets
**Profile:** Dallas, Atlanta, Nashville, Houston investors looking at expansion into Texas or diversifying capital. They care about which markets the municipal money is flowing into before prices adjust.
- **Where to find them:** LinkedIn (search: "multistate real estate investor" OR "Sun Belt investment"), syndication platforms, private equity real estate forums
- **Message angle:** $320M battery storage deal + November bond election = new infrastructure capital allocation shifting Austin's cost structure — this information is not priced in yet

### Segment C: Real Estate Coaches / Educational Operators
**Profile:** People who teach investors and want premium content to feed their audiences. They distribute through newsletters, courses, communities (A360-style groups). One coach can reach 50-200 qualified leads instantly.
- **Where to find them:** LinkedIn (search: "real estate investing coach"), podcast guests in the CRE space, Substack writers covering commercial real estate
- **Message angle:** Co-branded verified intelligence brief subscription — they get exclusive access before public release for their audience

### Segment D: Municipal / Entitlement Professionals
**Profile:** City planners, development consultants, legal professionals working directly with Austin's entitlement process. They already monitor council minutes but don't synthesize them into actionable investment theses.
- **Where to find them:** LinkedIn (search: "Austin development consultant" OR "land use attorney Texas"), AICP membership directory
- **Message angle:** Your clients need this intelligence — it's your data, packaged faster than they can digest council minutes themselves
