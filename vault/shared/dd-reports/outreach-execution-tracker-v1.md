## How to Use

1. Review the 10 archetype cards below
2. Pick 5 contacts from your network that match these archetypes
3. For each contact: find their name and one recent deal/fact (LinkedIn, CoStar, Loopnet — 2-3 min per person)
4. Fill in the `Name` field and replace the `[PERSONALIZATION_HOOK]` placeholder with one sentence about what you found
5. Hit send

**Total estimated time: 10-15 minutes for all 5 emails.**

**Personalization hook examples:**

## Response Handling Quick Reference

| Scenario | Response | Action |
|----------|----------|--------|
| "Send me a sample" | Interested | Send St. Johns demo link + ask for deal type (multifamily/fix&flip/dev) |
| "What's the cost?" | Price-sensitive | "It's free during beta — just honest feedback after you use it. Pricing starts around $497/report." |
| "We already have a tool" | Competitive | Ask what tool, thank them for the info. Move to Email 3 proof shot with specific differentiator (our system does X that they don't). |
| Not interested | No | Add to nurture list, revisit in 60 days. Send one final Email 3 as courtesy closure. |
| "Can you white-label this?" | Partnership signal | High-value lead. Ask who else at their organization would be involved, set up a 15-min call. |
