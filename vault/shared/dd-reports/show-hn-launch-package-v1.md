## Part C: HN Comment Strategy (Post-Launch)

### If someone asks "Why not just use Excel?":

> Excel works fine until it doesn't — and that's the whole problem. Spreadsheets are deterministic but error-prone (typos, broken formulas, version chaos). The alternative has been paid underwriters ($500+/report) or LLM-based tools that hallucinate confidently.
> 
> This sits between them: deterministic like Excel, automated like a tool. Every formula is visible and auditable. You can verify it yourself in 30 seconds.

### If someone asks "Is this just another AI tool?":

> Good question — the answer is no. There's no LLM processing calculations here. No "AI insights" that could be wrong. The underwriting engine uses fixed financial formulas (NPV, IRR, equity multiple, DSCR) computed deterministically from your inputs.
> 
> AI elements are limited to market context and narrative generation — things where uncertainty is acknowledged explicitly. But the numbers? Those are math. Not probability.

### If someone asks about pricing:

> The core engine is free (Chrome Extension + web demo). For full due diligence reports with market analysis, scenario modeling, and professional formatting, we're running a beta at $197–$497/report depending on complexity.
> 
> Building this because I needed it myself — the pricing was an afterthought.
