## Top Stories & Signals

### 1. AI advice made people three times less accurate but twice as confident (278 pts, 154 comments)
**Source:** TNW → study from French/Italian universities  
**Relevance: HIGH — Direct validation of deterministic underwriting positioning.**

Key findings:
- Willingness to say "I don't know" collapsed from 44% to 3% with AI advice available
- Accuracy dropped from 27% to 9% (one-third)
- Confidence rose from 30% to 76% (more than double)
- Monetary incentives barely moved the needle: accuracy only went to 16%

Wharton researchers previously coined "cognitive surrender" — people accept wrong AI answers 80% of the time while reporting higher confidence. This study is the sharpest data point yet.

**Positioning application:** This is the strongest deterministic/verifiable positioning signal we've captured. The narrative bridge is direct:

> "AI advice makes humans three times less accurate but twice as confident. In real estate, that's not a bug — it's a business model. Black-box AI tells investors to buy bad deals with absolute certainty. Our engine shows exactly why every number matters."

This should be the cornerstone of the next LinkedIn post and outreach messaging. It transforms from "we're more reliable" to "the alternative is literally making you worse at your job while convincing you that you're right."
