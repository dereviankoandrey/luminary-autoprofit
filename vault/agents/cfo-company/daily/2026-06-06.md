## Risk Factors & Downside Scenarios

### Macroeconomic Risks
1. **Interest rate environment:** If the Fed keeps rates at or above 4.5% through 2027, commercial real estate valuations compress 8%–15% across multifamily and mixed-use sectors. Buyer financing capacity drops dramatically.
2. **Recession scenario:** A US recession would hit Texas development markets harder than expected — not because of job loss (Texas has been resilient), but because construction lending dries up and institutional buyers pull back, extending time-to-sale from 6 months to 18+ months.
3. **Commercial real estate credit cycle:** Banks are tightening CRE lending standards. This reduces the pool of acquisition financing for potential buyers, compressing prices.

### Project-Specific Risks
4. **Entitlement delays:** If any project is not fully entitled, timelines extend by 6–18 months and carrying costs compound with no revenue offset.
5. **Capital stack fragility:** If the equity positions are highly leveraged (70%+ debt-to-value), even modest valuation declines could trigger covenant breaches or margin calls on construction loans.
6. **Single-country risk:** Nearly 100% of the defined portfolio is US/Texas-based. A Texas-specific economic shock (power grid failure, severe drought, major employer relocation) would impact most holdings simultaneously — no geographic hedge.
7. **Australia FX/tax overhang:** Melbourne assets add complexity that may not be worth the upside depending on current valuations and tax consequences of repatriation.
