## Best first buyers
Prioritize people with direct exposure to Austin timeline, permit, entitlement, or underwriting risk.

### Tier 1 — Best immediate fit
1. small and midsize Austin-area developers
2. acquisitions teams evaluating infill or multifamily opportunities
3. debt brokers or private lenders screening local development risk
4. land-use / development consultants who monitor city process changes for clients

### Tier 2 — Good fit after first proof
1. capital partners with Austin exposure
2. architects / civil firms that want a client-facing signal digest
3. owner-rep / project management groups

## Fast qualification rule
A lead is qualified if at least 3 of these are true:
- active in Austin or Greater Austin
- exposed to entitlement, permit, or development timing risk
- reviews deals, capital requests, or project feasibility regularly
- currently monitors city pages manually or inconsistently
- likely loses time or money when local process changes are missed
- can buy a low-ticket paid information product without committee friction

## Disqualifiers
Do not prioritize first:
- residential consumers
- general real estate news readers
- people outside Austin with no local workflow need
- buyers who only want bespoke property-by-property consulting

## Offer framing
**Core line:**
A weekly Austin entitlement-and-permit signal brief for operators underwriting local development risk.

**What they get:**
- 1 weekly issue
- 5-8 source-backed local signals
- operator implications under each signal
- watchlist of upcoming public process items
- source log

**Pilot pricing to test first:**
- Solo: $79/month
- Team: $199/month
- Founding-member angle: first 10 pilot subscribers keep pricing for 6 months

## Recommended first outreach sequence
1. send short permission-based message
2. if interested, send one-paragraph explanation plus 1 sample issue
3. ask one qualifying question about their workflow
4. offer paid pilot, not a long custom demo
5. log response as: interested / qualified / not now / not fit

## Copy-paste outreach templates

### 1) Short warm intro message
Hi [Name] — I’ve been building a compact weekly Austin development signal brief that tracks entitlement, permit, council, AHFC, and process changes that can affect underwriting and deal timing. I have two sample issues already done. Want me to send you one?

### 2) Follow-up after permission
Thanks — here’s the idea in one line:

It’s a paid weekly brief for Austin developers, lenders, and acquisition teams who want the local entitlement-and-permit signal layer without manually checking scattered city pages.

Each issue includes 5-8 source-backed signals, operator implications, and a short watchlist. If I send a sample, the main thing I’d want to know is whether this would actually save you time or help you catch process risk earlier.

### 3) Paid pilot offer message
I’m opening a small pilot for the Austin Entitlement Signal Brief.

Pilot structure:
- weekly issue
- 5-8 source-backed signals
- operator implications
- watchlist + source log

Pilot pricing is $79/month solo or $199/month team. First 10 can keep pilot pricing for 6 months.

Would you want in, want to see a sample first, or is this not relevant to your workflow?

### 4) “Not now” soft close
Understood. If timing changes, I can resend a sample later. If helpful, what would need to be true for this to become useful to you: more project-level linkage, broader market coverage, or just better timing?

## 3-question qualification script
Use after someone shows interest.

1. Are you actively looking at Austin development, acquisitions, lending, or entitlement-related work right now?
2. How are you currently monitoring local process or city-signal changes that could affect timelines or underwriting?
3. If a weekly brief solved that pain, would this be useful just for you, or for a broader team?

## What to listen for
Positive signs:
- “we currently check this manually”
- “we miss city/process updates unless someone flags them”
- “this could help screening / underwriting / internal updates”
- “send me the sample”
- “team might use this”

Weak signs:
- wants generic Austin market news
- wants one-off research on a specific parcel
- no Austin activity now
- no workflow owner for this problem

- 1 paid pilot or 2 serious follow-up conversations
