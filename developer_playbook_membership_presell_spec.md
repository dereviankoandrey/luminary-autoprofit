# Developer Playbook Membership — Presell Spec v1

## Purpose
Define the smallest recurring membership offer Luminary could validate before building a heavier content business.

## Core Offer
A monthly operator membership for small developers, acquisition leads, and technical real-estate operators who want practical workflows, templates, and agent-assisted execution playbooks instead of generic education.

## Candidate Deliverables
- 1 tactical playbook per week
- 1 reusable template / prompt / checklist per week
- monthly live or recorded teardown
- private archive of workflows grouped by topic

## Initial Positioning
Not "learn real estate" and not generic coaching.

Position as:
- operating system for lean developers
- field-tested prompts, underwriting workflows, diligence checklists, and automation playbooks
- saves time on execution, not just theory

## ICP
- small developers
- acquisition teams
- technical operators building internal automation
- independent sponsors who need repeatable systems

## Pricing Hypothesis
- founding member: $49-99/month
- team / partner tier: $199-299/month
- optional one-time setup or workshop only after recurring core offer proves demand

## Why It Could Work
- recurring library compounds over time
- information asymmetry improves if content is tied to real operator workflows
- low startup cost
- can be presold before full buildout

## Main Risks
- content treadmill
- trust depends on strong proof of usefulness
- can drift into consulting unless boundaries are tight
- retention may be weak if updates are too generic

## Minimum Autonomous Workflow
1. define 4 core content pillars
2. generate one-month starter backlog
3. turn each topic into a fixed playbook template
4. package as founding-member offer
5. test interest with one landing page and one checkout/waitlist path

## Suggested 4 Content Pillars
- underwriting and deal triage
- market intel and sourcing workflows
- diligence and execution checklists
- agent / automation operating playbooks

## Pass Standard
This idea earns promotion only if:
- one-page offer is clear in under 60 seconds,
- the first 4 weeks of content can be outlined in one sitting,
- and a founding-member presell feels plausible without custom promises.

## Smallest Next Test
Create a one-page founding-member offer with 4 sample playbook titles, pricing, and a simple join/waitlist CTA; judge whether the offer feels concrete or still too vague.
