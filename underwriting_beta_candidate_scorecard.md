# AI Underwriting SaaS — Beta Candidate Scorecard

**Last updated:** 2026-04-19 10:00 UTC  
**Purpose:** turn the beta target list into a sharper selection tool so Andrey can quickly choose the best 3-5 real people for first outreach.

## Why this exists
The current beta materials define:
- target segments
- outreach sequence
- demo flow
- success criteria

What was still missing was a simple way to rank real candidates before outreach starts. This scorecard solves that.

## Scoring Rule
Score each candidate from 1-5 on each dimension below.

- **Relationship proximity** — how warm is the path?
- **Reply likelihood** — how likely are they to respond quickly?
- **Pain intensity** — how strong is the likely screening/triage pain?
- **Workflow fit** — how closely does their deal flow match the current product?
- **Feedback quality** — how likely are they to give blunt, useful feedback?
- **Paid potential** — how likely are they to convert if value is proven?

**Total possible score:** 30

## Recommended Selection Rule
Prioritize candidates who are:
1. warm
2. fast to reply
3. in a recurring deal-screening workflow
4. capable of giving blunt practical feedback

If two candidates score similarly, pick the one with the faster and simpler path to a demo.

## First Outreach Batch Rule
For the first batch, choose:
- 2 candidates who are most likely to reply fast
- 2 candidates with the clearest product pain

- **Send now**
- **Send later**
- **Keep warm, not now**
