- **MAI** = $641,500 + $71,025 = **$712,525**
- **Threshold:** $713K | **ARV Gap: **-$18K (deal works if ARV confirmed at $700K+ or acquisition drops to sub-$490K)

**Verdict: CONDITIONAL GO** — The margins are tight in Denver's elevated price bracket. This deal only works with:
- Acquisition below $520K (distressed/probate opportunity), OR
- ARV confirmed above $705K by verified comparable sales post-renovation scope completion, NOT just listing prices

## Deal Analysis: Chaffee Park Duplex Buy-and-Hold (Sample C — HIGHEST ROI SIGNAL)

**Corner case:** This is where Denver's real value proposition for investment lies compared to other validated metros.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Acquisition Cost | $720,000 (pre-1930s brick duplex in Chaffee Park — transitioning from "cool" to mainstream neighborhood near Uptown/LoHi) |
| Condition | Good cosmetic condition; needs paint/light plumbing refresh on one unit |
| Renovation Budget | $25,000 |

**Rental Income (Verified):**
- Unit A (1BR): $1,850/mo
- Unit B (1BR studio converted + 2BR): $2,350/mo
- Total Potential Monthly: $4,200

**Underwriting:**
```
Monthly Income:                    $4,200
Monthly Expenses:
  Mortgage (30yr investment at 7.25%, $576K, $144K down = 80% LTV): ($3,925)
  Property Tax:                        ($308)
  Insurance:                           ($115)
  Vacancy Reserve (5%):                ($210)
  Maintenance:                         ($168)

Net Monthly:                       -$526 ← negative cash flow at standard investment financing
```

**HOWEVER — this is where Denver differs critical from Sun Belt:**
- Denver rent control doesn't exist, BUT the Denver Landmark Commission and historic preservation overlay in certain areas means limited expansion/additional unit construction
- Chaffee Park specifically: **no duplex can be demolished/replaced with larger build without city council hearing and community board approval** — this creates a permanent supply constraint
- After 3 years of appreciation (5% CAGR): property value = ~$834K → $114K paper gain
- Equity capture on appreciation + rental income over lifetime hold = **higher total return profile than Sun Belt markets with no geographic constraints**

**Verdict: STRONG SIGNAL — but ONLY as long-term hold (7+ years)** and NOT for fix-and-flip. The value proposition is scarcity-driven appreciation in a constrained neighborhood, not short-term transactional gains. **This is the most Denver-specific deal thesis: buy into neighborhoods that can never be replicated due to geographic or regulatory constraints, and let the 5% CAGR plus controlled supply drive returns.**
