# Austin Entitlement Signal Brief — Weekly Production SOP

## Purpose
Turn the Austin brief into a repeatable weekly product with minimal human time and high agent autonomy.

## Target output
- 1 weekly issue
- 5-8 signals
- 1 short executive summary
- 1 watchlist / operator actions section
- source log with dates checked

## Fixed source set
1. Council Meeting Information Center
2. Direct next City Council agenda URL
3. Direct next AHFC agenda URL
4. Austin Housing main page
5. Rental Assistance page
6. Commercial Plan Review page
7. Expedited Building Plan Review page
8. AHOST page

**Reliability note:** use the direct Council and AHFC agenda URLs as the primary weekly anchor. The meeting-center homepage is useful for navigation, but it may lag or emphasize recent meetings instead of the next decision window.

## 45-minute assembly workflow
### 1) Freshness check — 5 minutes
- Confirm the direct next council and AHFC agenda pages are live.
- Check whether the meeting center also reflects the same window or is lagging.
- Confirm at least one process page still loads.
- If a source fails, log fallback or replacement source.

### 2) Signal extraction — 15 minutes
Classify each fact into one of:
- capital allocation
- entitlement / code process
- permit workflow
- affordability / housing stress
- supply pipeline

Reject anything that is:
- generic civic news with no operator implication
- stale with no current action window
- not tied to Austin development, housing, or permitting

### 3) Prioritization — 10 minutes
Score each signal on:
- operator relevance
- time sensitivity
- uniqueness / information asymmetry
- ability to infer action

Keep the top 5-8 signals only.

### 4) Issue drafting — 10 minutes
For each signal include:
- fact
- why it matters
- operator implication
- impact level

### 5) QA — 5 minutes
- verify dates
- verify dollar amounts
- remove duplicated implications
- keep tone operator-grade, not newsletter-fluffy
- confirm every signal has a source

## Standard issue skeleton
1. headline
2. executive summary
3. top signals
4. process / capital / housing watchlist
5. underwriting implications
6. near-term operator triggers
7. source log
8. validation readout

## Human involvement
- week 1: 1-2 hours to review positioning and approve pricing
- ongoing: 15-30 minutes issue QA if desired

## Automation notes
- The brief is strongest when anchored to meeting cadence plus one or two concrete agenda items.
- AHFC agenda items are high-value because they create specific, monetizable local capital signals.
- AHOST is better as a slower reconciliation layer than a weekly lead source.

## Pass criteria
A weekly issue is production-ready if it can be assembled from the fixed source set in under 45 minutes, with at least 5 operator-relevant signals and no external outreach.
