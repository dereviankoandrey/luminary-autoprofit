## Message routing rule
- **Cell A:** use the direct paid-pilot script from `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_pricing_test_pack.md`
- **Cell B:** use permission-based sample-first outreach from `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_launch_pack.md`

## Asset selection rule
Pick one primary proof asset per contact.

Default from `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_asset_routing_sheet.md`:
- **landing / one-pager** if they need quick comprehension
- **sample issue** if they need proof of substance
- **agenda teaser / proof asset** if they need trust / source continuity
- **pilot offer / founding framing** if they are already close to the buying decision

Do not send everything at once.

## Pre-send checklist for each contact
- Why are they a fit right now?
- Which exact pain are we testing?
- Which cell are they in?
- Which single proof asset goes first?
- Who should send it?
- When is the follow-up date if no reply?

## Same-day send workflow
1. Fill all 5 rows.
2. Confirm message owner and best channel.
3. Choose one proof asset per person.
4. Send the first 2-3 messages.
5. Log status immediately in `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_outreach_tracker.md`.
6. Send the remaining 2 if the first sends look clean.

## Reply handling shortcut
- **Interested:** move to `sample sent` or `pilot offered`
- **Relevant but unclear:** ask the workflow-fit question from the pricing test pack
- **No reply:** follow up once after 5-7 business days
- **Not now:** log reason and next date
- **Not fit:** remove from active batch

## Minimum success for batch 1
Any of these count as useful signal:
- 2 replies
- 1 pricing-positive reply
- 1 clear workflow-validation conversation
