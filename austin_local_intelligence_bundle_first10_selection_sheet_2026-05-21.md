## Selection rule
Pick people who are:
1. active in Austin-area development, lending, acquisitions, or land-use workflow
2. likely to feel both entitlement uncertainty and permit / review friction
3. warm enough to message without awkward cold outreach
4. able to say yes to a low-ticket pilot without a long committee process

## Fast scoring rubric
Score each candidate 0-2 on each line.

- **Austin exposure** — 0 none, 1 indirect, 2 direct/current
- **Workflow pain** — 0 unclear, 1 possible, 2 obvious
- **Warmth** — 0 weak, 1 moderate, 2 strong
- **Buying ability** — 0 unlikely, 1 maybe, 2 likely
- **Speed** — 0 slow, 1 medium, 2 fast

**Total:** /10

## Priority rule
- **8-10:** send in batch 1
- **6-7:** send in batch 2
- **0-5:** hold unless list is thin
