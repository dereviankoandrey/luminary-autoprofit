## Our Differentiation Matrix

### What Competitors Do Right
- **Real Estate Profitability Analyzer & Deal Analyzer:** Prove the market exists — users want instant math-based analysis on Zillow. Both have perfect or near-perfect ratings, indicating user satisfaction with deterministic tools.
- **CashOn:** Shows paid subscription model is viable in this category (external site dependency = recurring revenue).
- **HomeLens:** Multi-platform support (Zillow + Redfin + Realtor.com) works — but their 4.0 rating vs our math-only approach suggests AI features may be a net negative for trust.

### Where We Win — Clear Unoccupied Positioning

| Dimension | Competitors | Deal Screener |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Analysis methodology** | Either basic cap rate OR AI/LLM-based | Full underwriting engine (fix-and-flip, multifamily, development) with deterministic math |
| **Platform support** | Most Zillow-only; HomeLens does 3 sites but is AI-reliant | Zillow + Redfin built-in, proven selectors |
| **Pricing model** | Free tools are limited; paid tools require subscriptions | Free forever (client-side), optional email capture for upsell |
| **Trust signal** | ".ai" branding signals black-box uncertainty | "No LLMs. No hallucinations." — anti-AI-slop positioning aligns with current HN sentiment |
| **Server costs** | Most competitors have infrastructure (AI API calls, cloud hosting) | Zero server cost = sustainable free tier |
| **Multi-analyser** | Single-purpose calculators only | Three analyser types in one extension |

### The Gap Nobody's Occupying
**"Professional-grade underwriting that runs locally for free."**

Every competitor is either:
1. A simple calculator (cap rate + NOI) — not real underwriting
2. An AI tool with ".ai" branding — the exact anti-pattern we're positioned against

We're the only product offering **full deal analysis (not just cap rates)** that's both **deterministic** AND **free**. This is a defensible moat because:
- Simple calculators can't compete on depth
- AI tools can't compete on trust or cost structure
