# Experiment: AI Disclosure Compliance Checklist

**Date:** 2026-07-19  
**Lane:** New product concept (complements existing DD Reports lane)  
**Status:** Autonomous build complete — awaiting human distribution decision  

## Signal Triggered
NYC Mayor Mamdani's Rental Ripoff Report requiring disclosure of AI-altered property listing imagery. First municipal regulation of its kind in real estate.

## Product Concept
A free downloadable compliance checklist for real estate professionals, designed to:
- Capture leads via email (free download)
- Position Andrey as a regulatory-aware operator (not just a tool builder)
- Create natural upsell path to DD Reports ("If you need help verifying your underwriting inputs, our deterministic engine produces auditable outputs")

## Assets Produced
1. **AI-DISCLOSURE-CHECKLIST-v1.md** — The actual product (comprehensive checklist covering imagery, financial projections, tenant communications, and internal processes)
2. **linkedin-post-mamdani-ai-images-real-estate-2026-07-19.md** — LinkedIn post promoting the concept

## Key Risks
1. **Regulatory momentum may be NYC-specific** — other jurisdictions may not follow quickly
2. **Lead quality uncertainty** — compliance checklist downloaders may not be active deal-makers
3. **Positioning risk** — if the checklist is too generic, it won't convert to DD Reports

| Action | Time | Frequency |
|--------|------|-----------|

| Respond to DMs | 10 min per lead | Ongoing while post is active |
| Convert to PDF + distribute | 10 min | One-time setup |

## Validation Criteria (How We Know It Works)
- **Signal 1:** LinkedIn post gets >50 reactions or >20 comments within 48 hours
- **Signal 2:** ≥3 DMs with "DD" or similar opt-in within first week
- **Signal 3:** ≥1 DD Report conversion from checklist leads within 30 days
- **Kill switch:** If <2 DMs in first week, pivot to different content angle

## Why This Is the Best Autonomous Experiment Tonight
1. **Zero capital** — no spend required
2. **No external messaging** — Andrey controls all outbound (LinkedIn is his account)
3. **Irreversible spend: none** — if it flops, we've spent 0 dollars and gained market intelligence about regulatory interest levels
4. **Fast validation cycle** — LinkedIn engagement metrics give us signal within 24-48 hours
5. **Compounding value** — the checklist becomes a reusable asset for future lead generation; content can be repurposed into blog posts, newsletter segments, or even a paid compliance guide

## Recommendation
This experiment should run parallel to existing lane activation (Chrome Extension submission + Underwriting SaaS deploy). The compliance checklist is a CONTENT play that feeds INTO the other lanes' distribution — it's not competing for Andrey's time, it's amplifying his existing assets.
