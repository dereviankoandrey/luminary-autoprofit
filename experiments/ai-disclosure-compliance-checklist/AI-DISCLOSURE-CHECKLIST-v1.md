## Checklist

### Section 1: Property Listing Imagery

- [ ] All photos are original captures (no AI staging or enhancement) — OR disclosure statement included
- [ ] Virtual staging is disclosed in listing description with label "virtually staged"
- [ ] Skipped/damaged items removed via AI tools are noted in property condition section
- [ ] Floor plans generated from AI analysis include source attribution and accuracy disclaimer
- [ ] 3D/virtual tour renders disclose any AI-generated elements

**Disclosure template (copy/paste):** "Some images in this listing have been enhanced or virtually staged using AI tools. Original photographs are available upon request."

### Section 2: Financial Projections & Marketing Materials

- [ ] All ROI projections include methodology documentation
- [ ] Market comparison data cites source and date
- [ ] Any AI-generated market analysis includes confidence level disclosure
- [ ] Pro forma assumptions are explicitly stated (rent growth, vacancy rate, expense ratios)
- [ ] No AI-generated "comparable sales" without verified MLS/source confirmation

**Disclosure template:** "Financial projections in this offering use modeled assumptions. All inputs and methodologies are available for review upon request."

### Section 3: Tenant & Buyer Communications

- [ ] Email templates disclose automated/AI-assisted generation where applicable
- [ ] Property condition disclosures differentiate between observed conditions and AI-suggested assessments
- [ ] Lease documents reviewed by legal counsel (not solely AI-generated)
- [ ] Automated responses include human follow-up guarantee with timeline

### Section 4: Internal Processes

- [ ] Team has documented policy on acceptable AI use in client-facing materials
- [ ] Annual compliance review scheduled
- [ ] Staff trained on disclosure requirements for current and pending regulations
- [ ] Client communication protocol includes transparency statement about AI tools used in analysis
