📖 What Is BRRRR?

BRRRR stands for Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat. It's a real estate investment strategy where you:

1. Buy — Purchase a distressed property below market value
2. Rehab — Renovate to increase the property's value (ARV)
3. Rent — Place a qualified tenant and generate cash flow
4. Refinance — Obtain a new mortgage based on the higher ARV, pulling out your original capital
5. Repeat — Use the recycled capital to acquire another property

The magic of BRRRR is that you can potentially recycle 100%+ of your initial investment, allowing you to scale a rental portfolio with minimal ongoing capital outlay. This calculator helps you model each step and determine if a deal meets your criteria for a successful BRRRR strategy.
