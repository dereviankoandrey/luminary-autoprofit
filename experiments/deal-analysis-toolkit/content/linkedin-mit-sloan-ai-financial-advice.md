MIT just published a study showing AI financial advice is "surprisingly good" — but only when you ask structured questions.

Half of Americans now use AI for financial decisions. MIT researchers tested GPT-5.2, GPT-5.6, and Gemini 3 Flash across 1,000 adults over simulated lifetimes. The results are fascinating:

LLMs consistently advised smart behavior — save during working years, draw down in retirement, invest heavily in diversified stock funds. But when researchers introduced structured academic prompts with full financial information (age, income, savings balances, economic assumptions), the quality improved dramatically.
