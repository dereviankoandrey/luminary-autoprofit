## How to Use This Toolkit

1. Pick any deal — fix-and-flip, buy-and-hold (single-family or multifamily), land acquisition
2. Run it through ALL three tools in order: Score → Finance → Due Diligence
3. If the Deal Scoring Matrix returns NO-GO with any category, move on unless you can demonstrate material negotiation impact on the red flag
4. Cross-reference Due Diligence findings with every scoring line item — discrepancies mean adjust your assumptions, not your thresholds

## Deterministic vs Gut-Feel: Why This Matters

| Approach | Decision Quality | Repeatability | Partner Confidence | Risk of Blind Spots |
|----------|------------------|---------------|---------------------|--------------------|
| "Feels right" (gut) | Subjectivity wins | Can't explain to anyone later | Low — no paper trail | High — what you don't see destroys deals |
| Calculator dump (Excel-only) | Numbers feel like proof but assumptions are hidden | Spreadsheet lives on one person's machine | Medium — can audit the formulas if they share it | Medium-High — assumptions baked into cells go unverified |
| **This Toolkit** | Deterministic thresholds, auditable in plain language | Any device, printable for field use, portable PDF/Markdown | High — anyone reads scores and sees exactly why | Low — six-phase checklist catches everything |
