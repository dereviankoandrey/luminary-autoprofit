## Composite Financing Recommendation: Hybrid A → D Pathway

**Phase 1 — Acquisition:** Use **Option C (HELOC)** for purchase close ($58K closing costs + immediate acquisition draw) if credit approval timeline permits (<7 days). This is critical because seller's counteroffers only expire within 48–72 hours of an offer; HELOC provides the fastest capital deployment to secure contracts in Austin's competitive market.

**Phase 2 — Reno Execution:** Transition **Option A (Hard Money)** once deal closes and purchase is recorded on title — the same lender can convert to a fix-and-flip loan covering reno draws, replacing HELOC exposure within months of closing when ARV increases justify higher borrowing limits. Or use Option D if JV partner found before close — which provides lower monthly cash burn but requires upfront negotiation time (10–20 business days for term sheet and legal docs).

**Option B works when:** Family/friends explicitly offer capital at favorable terms AND Andrey doesn't want to risk personal home equity. This is actually the most practical "first-time investor" pathway where relationship trust replaces credit qualifications.
