**Version:** 1.0 | **Updated:** 2026-07-29  
**Author:** Andrey Derevianko (20-year developer/CFO, $4B+ portfolio operator)  
**Philosophy:** Your capital stack is your competitive advantage. Optimize it like a CFO — not a borrower.

---

## How to Use This Template

1. List every financing option you're considering for the deal
2. Fill in all terms from each lender/source (get them IN WRITING)
3. Run the cost-of-capital comparison across your projected hold period
4. Calculate total cash required and total cost of capital
5. Choose the optimal stack based on total cost, flexibility, and risk

**Rule #1:** If a lender won't give you terms in writing, they're not financing — they're fishing for your business plan. Move to the next option.

**Red flags to watch for:**
- [ ] Lender won't provide terms in writing within 48 hours
- [ ] "We'll work it out" instead of documented draw schedule
- [ ] Personal guarantee on a non-recourse deal (contradiction)
- [ ] Prepayment penalty exceeds 6 months (negotiate down or walk)
- [ ] Inspection frequency > weekly (operational burden is real)
