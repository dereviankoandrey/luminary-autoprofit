# Austin Bundle -> Opportunity Analysis Bridge

**Status:** revenue-bridge asset  
**Created:** 2026-05-21 10:00 UTC cycle

## Purpose
Turn the Austin Local Intelligence Bundle into a feeder for the higher-ticket **Real Estate Opportunity Analysis** offer.

This closes the current strategy gap:
- the Austin bundle is the strongest low-capital, recurring, agent-run product
- the Opportunity Analysis offer is still the fastest path to near-term cash

The goal is to use the bundle to surface paid workflow pain, then move the right buyers into:
- **Quick Take**: $250-$500
- **Full Opportunity Analysis**: $1,000-$2,500+

## Why this matters
Right now the business has two strong lanes, but they are not linked tightly enough.

- **Lane 1:** Austin bundle proves recurring relevance and gives a reason to start conversations.
- **Lane 2:** Opportunity Analysis is the higher-ticket service most likely to produce first revenue quickly.

Without a bridge, bundle interest risks stopping at low-ticket curiosity.
With a bridge, bundle conversations can convert into project-specific paid work.

## Core logic
The bundle tells a buyer:
- what changed
- where Austin timing risk is moving
- which process shifts may affect underwriting or sequencing

The Opportunity Analysis then answers:
- what this means for **your specific site, deal, or concept**
- whether to pursue, reshape, delay, or kill the opportunity

That makes the upgrade path natural instead of forced.

## Best bridge moments
Move from bundle to analysis when a prospect says or implies any of these:

1. "How would this affect our site / deal?"
2. "Can you look at a specific property?"
3. "We are evaluating something in Austin right now."
4. "Timeline risk is what we care about most."
5. "Can you help us think through entitlement or execution risk?"
6. "This is useful, but we need something more specific than a weekly signal brief."

## Offer ladder

### Step 1 — Start with the bundle
Use the Austin Local Intelligence Bundle as:
- a paid pilot
- a proof asset
- a conversation starter
- a way to identify buyers with active Austin exposure

### Step 2 — Qualify for project-specific pain
Once relevance is confirmed, ask:
- Are you looking at an active Austin site or development path right now?
- Is the main concern timing, entitlement risk, concept fit, or execution sequencing?
- Would an outside operator read on the specific opportunity be more useful than a general market signal layer?

### Step 3 — Route to the right paid service
- **Quick Take** when the buyer wants a fast outside read before going deeper
- **Full Opportunity Analysis** when the buyer needs a more structured decision memo

## Qualification rules

### Route to Quick Take when
- one site or deal is active now
- the buyer needs fast screening
- the decision is early-stage
- the main need is "sanity check this before we spend more time"

### Route to Full Opportunity Analysis when
- the project is more complex
- multiple risk layers exist
- the buyer wants a decision-support memo
- there is meaningful capital, timing, or concept risk riding on the choice

## Bridge scripts

### 1. Soft bridge after bundle interest
This is exactly the kind of workflow the bundle is meant to support.

If it would help, I also do a project-specific **Real Estate Opportunity Analysis** for people who want an outside operator read on a live site, concept, or deal before they commit more time or money.

That can be either a fast **Quick Take** or a deeper **Full Opportunity Analysis**, depending on the decision you are trying to make.

### 2. Quick Take route
If the real question is "Should we spend more time on this opportunity, and what are we likely missing?" the best fit is usually a **Quick Take**.

That is a concise outside read on the specific site or deal, focused on upside, red flags, entitlement / execution considerations, and the smartest next move.

### 3. Full Analysis route
If you want a more structured judgment call on the actual opportunity, I can do a **Full Opportunity Analysis**.

That is a deeper memo covering upside drivers, major risks, development logic, entitlement / execution considerations, key unknowns, and recommended next steps.

### 4. Permission-based transition question
Would it be more useful for me to keep sending the Austin signal layer, or to look at a specific opportunity you are evaluating right now?
