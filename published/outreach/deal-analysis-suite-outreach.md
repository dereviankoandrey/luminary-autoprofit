## The Pitch (Elevator Version)

> "Right now, every real estate deal you evaluate takes one of two paths: a quick napkin math that misses real risks, or a full analyst review that costs $5K+ and takes weeks. We've built an autonomous system that does both — instant underwriting with your decision thresholds, followed by a 5-10 page due diligence report that covers scenario analysis, risk assessment, and actionable next steps. Think of it as having a senior underwriter + DD analyst in your workflow, running on AI, available instantly."

## Target Segments (Prioritized)

1. **Multifamily operators doing value-add** — highest deal frequency, most natural fit for the full workflow
2. **Private lenders / hard money operators** — evaluate deals daily, need fast GO/NO-GO screens with clear risk assessment
3. **Syndicators / GPs raising capital** — need professional-grade DD reports to include in offering memorandums
4. **Development companies in Austin/South Texas** — Permit Velocity Monitor is specifically relevant here
