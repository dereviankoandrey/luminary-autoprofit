## Tweet 1/5
A new study found something terrifying about AI and decision-making:

When people used AI advice for financial decisions:
• Accuracy dropped from 27% → 9% (3x worse)
• "I don't know" responses collapsed 44% → 3%
• Confidence rose from 30% → 76% (2x higher)

They didn't just get wrong answers. They became worse at recognizing they were wrong. 🧵

---

## Tweet 2/5
Wharton researchers call this "cognitive surrender."

The AI doesn't just give bad advice — it degrades your ability to question it. The more confident the AI sounds, the less likely you are to double-check.

This is dangerous for anyone making financial decisions with AI tools.
