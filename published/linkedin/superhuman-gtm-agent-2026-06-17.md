1Mind is a useful case study: the product isn't the avatar. It's the *GTM Brain*.

The real shift is from chatbots to customer‑facing agents that can qualify, book, and guide buyers with memory, tool calls, and deterministic guardrails. The face is optional. The moat is the owned brain: private knowledge ingestion, CRM/tool orchestration, customer context, and workflow control.

If you're building in enterprise AI, the question isn't "Do we need an avatar?" It's "Who owns the brain?"

Voice\/waveform first. Face later. Private stack before scale.
