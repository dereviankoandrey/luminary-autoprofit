## The Problem (For Outreach Messaging)

Real estate investors waste hours or thousands of dollars evaluating each deal:
- **Spreadsheet underwriting** is fast but misses risks that require context, market data, and nuanced analysis
- **Analyst reports** are thorough but cost $2K–$15K per deal and take days — by then the deal may be gone
- **Commercial tools** (PropStream, BatchLeads) give leads and basic comps but don't tell you whether a specific deal pencils under your thresholds
- Most investors end up doing BOTH: their own spreadsheet math + paying an analyst for a report. That's double the cost, double the time, and two different conclusions to reconcile.

## The Solution (Three Layers)

### Layer 1: Underwriting SaaS — Instant GO/NO-GO Screen
Enter deal parameters → get instant recommendation with exact metrics (cap rate, DSCR, CoC, IRR) against your personal thresholds. Covers fix-and-flip, multifamily, development, mixed-use, and SFR. Same framework that professional underwriters use internally.

**Deliverable:** 1-page screen, ~30 seconds.  
**Value:** Eliminates the "should I even bother?" question in under a minute.

### Layer 2: Due Diligence Reports — Deep Analysis on Promising Deals
When the quick screen says GO (or REVIEW), trigger a full DD report: financial scenarios (base/adverse/optimistic), risk assessment with specific mitigations, comparable sales context, and a prioritized checklist of verifications before making an offer.

**Deliverable:** 5-10 page structured report, ~30 minutes agent processing.  
**Value:** Replaces $2K–$5K analyst reports for individual deal evaluation. Produces investor-grade output in minutes, not days.

### Layer 3: Market Intelligence — Austin (expanding to other markets)
Permit velocity tracking, entitlement signal monitoring, zoning change alerts. Information that developers need before feasibility assumptions go stale.

**Deliverable:** Recurring briefs with actionable early-warning signals.  
**Value:** Asymmetric intelligence from publicly available data that most investors don't know exists or don't have time to extract.

## Why This Converts Better Than Individual Products

1. **Workflow, not tool:** Investors don't want "another tool." They want their deal evaluation process to work better. The Suite positions as an end-to-end workflow solution.
2. **Clear value ladder:** Quick screen → deep dive is the natural decision flow investors already follow (just manually). This automates both steps.
3. **Reduced friction:** Bundle pricing with upfront commitment removes the "let me try each separately" paralysis. The bundle is cheaper than buying the equivalent individual products.
4. **Moonshot credibility:** An autonomous system producing investor-grade analysis demonstrates real agentic capability — not another spreadsheet wrapper. This resonates with sophisticated operators who are watching the AI landscape closely.
