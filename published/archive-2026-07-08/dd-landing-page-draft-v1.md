## What's Included in Every Report

- **Full financial breakdown** — all metrics calculated explicitly with labeled formulas: ROI, IRR, equity multiple, cap rate, DSCR, and every threshold your deal passes or fails
- **Scenario analysis** — base case, optimistic, and adverse scenarios so you see the range of possible outcomes, not just a best-case fantasy
- **Risk assessment & action items** — prioritized checklist of what to investigate next (and which risks are deal-breakers), with specific steps you can execute immediately

## How It Works

1. **Submit your deal details** — Fill out a short intake form with property type, location, acquisition price, construction budget, ARV, financing terms, and any comp data you have. Takes about 5 minutes.

2. **Receive your report in <24 hours** — Our agent-powered analysis generates a structured 8-section DD report covering financials, scenarios, risks, and a clear recommendation. Human reviewer quality-checks all calculations.

3. **Make an informed decision** — Get a definitive GO / NO-GO / CONDITIONAL verdict with confidence score, specific action items, and transparent math you can verify yourself. No jargon. No ambiguity.

## Why This Exists

Institutional real estate firms have had this process for decades: structured due diligence frameworks that evaluate every deal against hard financial thresholds before capital moves. The problem? Those tools are expensive, slow, and built for portfolios with hundreds of deals — not for the individual investor or small team evaluating one property at a time.

This service distills institutional-grade analytical rigor into a fast, affordable report you can actually use. Every calculation is shown. Every assumption is stated. Every risk is flagged. What you don't get is marketing fluff pretending every deal looks good. If a deal fails the math, the report says so.
