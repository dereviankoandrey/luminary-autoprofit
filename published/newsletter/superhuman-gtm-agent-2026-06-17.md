# Newsletter Section: The GTM Brain Over the Avatar

## Key Insight: The Real Product Isn&#x27;t an Avatar—It&#x27;s a &#x201C;Superhuman GTM Agent&#x201D; with a Private-Owned Brain

1Mind&#x27;s practical product is a customer-facing agent with presence, voice, memory, and sales workflows. The visual face increases perceived capability, but the defensibility sits in the GTM Brain: context graph, guardrails, workflows, and tool orchestration.

## Why This Matters for Enterprise

- **Enterprise focus:** 1Mind sells enterprise/mid-market B2B (not SMB)
- **ACVs:** $100K-$400K
- **$1M contracted revenue** in ~3 months
- **211% NDR** reported

## The Buildable Wedge

1. **Voice/waveform first, face optional**
2. **Private/owned GTM Brain with deterministic guardrails**
3. **Tavus for face validation, Calendly for booking, CRM connectors for context**

## Action: Start with single-tenant, private brain proving latency/quality/conversion before scaling to multi-tenant or face-dependent versions.

## Newsletter Insight

The 1Mind case reveals a pattern: **focus on the GTM brain, not the avatar.** Build your own context graph, guardrails, and workflows first. Validate with voice/waveform UIs. Add faces later if they drive meaningful conversion lift.

Real estate development projects can benefit from the same approach: private agent for buyer qualification, meeting booking, and CRM notes without compromising data ownership.
