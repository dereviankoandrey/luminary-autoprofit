### 2. Austin is teeing up a citywide density-bonus rewrite that would replace DB90 and VMU
- **Source:** May 21, 2026 City Council agenda, item 56
- **Fact:** Council is set to consider an ordinance amending Title 25 to create new zoning districts for a **citywide density bonus program** allowing additional height, modified uses, site-development regulations, and compatibility changes in exchange for affordable housing and other community benefits; the item would **replace DB90 and VMU**.
- **Why it matters:** this is a major underwriting and land-value signal, not a marginal code tweak.
- **Operator implication:** any Austin multifamily or commercial pipeline exposed to current DB90/VMU assumptions may need repricing, redesign, or faster pre-positioning.
- **Impact:** very high

### 3. A 45-year development agreement covering roughly 2,614 acres in Dog's Head is on the agenda
- **Source:** May 21, 2026 City Council agenda, item 38
- **Fact:** Council is set to authorize negotiation and execution of a **45-year development agreement** for approximately **2,614 acres** in the area generally known as **Dog's Head**, bounded by the Colorado River, US 183, and SH 130.
- **Why it matters:** very large-area development agreements are long-duration supply, land-use, and infrastructure signals.
- **Operator implication:** track Dog's Head as a long-horizon growth and entitlement node rather than waiting for downstream parcel-by-parcel noise.
- **Impact:** very high

### 4. South Lamar’s SoLa Mixed Use project is seeking a Barton Springs Zone redevelopment exception with a quantified mitigation fee
- **Source:** May 21, 2026 City Council agenda, item 46
- **Fact:** Council is set to consider a **Barton Springs Zone redevelopment exception** for the proposed **SoLa Mixed Use** development at South Lamar / Skyway Circle addresses tied to site plan **SP-2024-0397C**, with a stated mitigation fee of **$535,381.23**.
- **Why it matters:** this is exactly the kind of named entitlement signal operators pay for: specific project, specific geography, specific regulatory pathway, specific mitigation cost.
- **Operator implication:** institutionalize Barton Springs Zone exception items as premium watchlist triggers for South Austin redevelopment underwriting.
- **Impact:** high

### 5. Density-bonus administration is also being tightened at the permitting/process layer
- **Source:** May 21, 2026 City Council agenda, item 35
- **Fact:** Council is set to amend density bonus and incentive program requirements in Chapter 4-18 to clarify tenant organizing rights, allow some applications to be processed before the land-use restriction agreement is signed, allow fee waivers, and require an on-site professional relocation specialist.
- **Why it matters:** even before the big zoning rewrite, Austin is changing how affordability-linked development programs are administered.
- **Operator implication:** affordable or bonus-driven projects may face both new flexibility and new compliance burden; sponsors should review relocation and LURA assumptions early.
- **Impact:** high

### 6. Transit-related development review rules are also in motion
- **Source:** May 21, 2026 City Council agenda, item 55
- **Fact:** Council is set to consider an ordinance repealing and amending Title 25 provisions affecting **development review processes, water-quality compliance methods, street design, and utility infrastructure design** for certain transit system projects and related infrastructure.
- **Why it matters:** changes to transit-project review rules can ripple into corridor assumptions, adjacent entitlements, and infrastructure timing.
- **Operator implication:** any land thesis tied to Austin transit corridors should monitor this item and supporting documents, not just zoning cases.
- **Impact:** medium-high

### 7. East Riverside remains active at both corridor-plan and project-specific levels
- **Source:** May 21, 2026 City Council agenda, items 57 and 63 (draft agenda extraction)
- **Fact:** Council is set to consider amending the **East Riverside Corridor Regulating Plan** to remove **1404 East Riverside Drive** from the plan boundary, while the same agenda also carries a project-specific rezoning / PUD-related East Riverside item.
- **Why it matters:** that combination signals live corridor-level change plus parcel-level entitlement movement.
- **Operator implication:** East Riverside should remain a standing premium submarket watchlist in the brief.
- **Impact:** high
