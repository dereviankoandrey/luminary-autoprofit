7. log every reply and objection
8. pause after 5 tests and review pricing/relevance pattern

## Status definitions
- **queued** — name selected, not sent
- **sent** — first outreach sent
- **sample sent** — proof sent, awaiting reaction
- **qualified** — clear workflow fit confirmed
- **pilot offered** — direct pricing/pilot ask made
- **won** — agreed to pilot
- **not now** — timing issue, possible later follow-up
- **not fit** — poor ICP or wrong workflow

## Cell guide
- **A** — direct paid pilot
- **B** — sample-first
- **C** — founding-member framing
