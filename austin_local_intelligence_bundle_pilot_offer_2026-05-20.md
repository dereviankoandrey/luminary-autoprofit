# Austin Local Intelligence Bundle — Pilot Offer

**Status:** AutoProfit validation asset  
**Created:** 2026-05-20 02:03 UTC cycle

## Offer
A compact recurring Austin development-intelligence subscription that combines:
- the **Austin Entitlement Signal Brief**
- a **Permit Velocity Monitor** section

The goal is to give developers, lenders, acquisition teams, and consultants one operator-ready weekly view of:
- council and entitlement signals
- permit-timeline drift
- review-process changes
- expedited-review implications
- near-term watchlist items that could affect underwriting, deal timing, or land strategy

## Why bundle it
- The buyer set is the same across both products.
- The permit-monitor section increases practical utility without requiring a separate sales motion.
- Bundling reduces the risk that the permit product is viewed as too narrow on its own.
- The workflow still stays low-cost because both products use the same official Austin source stack.

## Pilot scope
Subscribers would receive:
1. **1 weekly issue** with 5-8 Austin entitlement and policy signals
2. **1 permit-velocity section** covering current site-plan metrics, dashboard changes, and expedited-review implications
3. **operator implications** under each major item
4. **next-cycle watchlist** for upcoming council and process changes
5. **source log** with official links

## Positioning
Not a generic local newsletter.

This is the **Austin development risk-and-velocity signal layer** for operators exposed to entitlement timing and permit friction.

## Who it is for
- developers
- acquisition teams
- lenders and debt brokers
- entitlement and land-use consultants
- landowners or capital partners with active Austin exposure

## Pilot pricing hypothesis
- **Solo pilot:** $99/month
- **Team pilot:** $249/month
- optional founding-member anchor: first 10 subscribers keep pilot pricing for 6 months

## Revenue logic
- This can start as a high-margin information product.
- The bundle creates a cleaner path to upsell later into custom analysis, portfolio monitoring, or software.
- If buyers only value the entitlement section, the permit layer can remain a differentiating module instead of a standalone SKU.

## Promise boundaries
This is **not** legal advice, engineering advice, or project-specific due diligence.
It is a compact recurring intelligence layer assembled from official public Austin sources.
