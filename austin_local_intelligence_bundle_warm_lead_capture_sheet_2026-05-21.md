# Austin Local Intelligence Bundle — Warm Lead Capture Sheet

**Status:** execution asset  
**Created:** 2026-05-21 05:00 UTC cycle

## Purpose
Make it easy to brain-dump 15-20 warm Austin-relevant names before scoring the first 10 for the bundled offer.

Use this before:
- `austin_local_intelligence_bundle_first10_selection_sheet_2026-05-21.md`
- `austin_local_intelligence_bundle_outreach_tracker_2026-05-21.md`

## Why this exists
The bundle is already packaged well enough to test.

The current blocker is getting a credible first list of warm names who feel both entitlement risk and permit / review friction.

This sheet is designed to remove blank-page delay.

## 15-minute capture rule
Do this quickly.

1. Set a 15-minute timer.
2. List names without scoring yet.
3. Favor warm and relevant over impressive.
4. Stop once 15-20 names are listed.
5. Then move the strongest names into the selection sheet.

## Best buckets to check
### Bucket 1 — Developers and acquisitions
Think of:
- Austin-area developers
- multifamily or mixed-use operators
- acquisitions people screening land, infill, or entitlement-heavy deals
- owner-operators who care about timing drift and city-process risk

### Bucket 2 — Lenders and debt brokers
Think of:
- people screening local development loans
- debt brokers who see weak timelines early
- lenders who care about entitlement and permit timing variance

### Bucket 3 — Land-use and development consultants
Think of:
- zoning / entitlement consultants
- civil, planning, or owner-rep contacts with developer clients
- project-management groups exposed to review-process friction

### Bucket 4 — Capital and adjacent operators
Think of:
- capital partners with Austin development exposure
- architects or engineers who may value a client-facing signal digest
- adjacent operators who still feel the cost of permitting delays

## Fast memory prompts
Use any prompt that helps names surface.

- Who has complained about Austin review delays or city friction before?
- Who regularly asks what council, process, or permit changes mean?
- Who screens Austin deals often enough to care about timeline drift?
- Who could say yes to a low-ticket paid pilot without heavy committee process?
- Who is warm enough that a short message would feel natural?
- Who would give blunt useful feedback even if they do not buy?

## Conversion rule after capture
1. Delete obvious non-fits.
2. Move the best 10 names into `austin_local_intelligence_bundle_first10_selection_sheet_2026-05-21.md`.
3. Score them there.
4. Move batch 1 names into `austin_local_intelligence_bundle_outreach_tracker_2026-05-21.md`.
5. Send only the best 5 first.

A fast imperfect list is better than waiting for the perfect 10.
