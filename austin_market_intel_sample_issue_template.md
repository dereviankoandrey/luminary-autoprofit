# Austin Market Intel Subscription — Sample Issue Template v1

## Purpose
Create one repeatable issue format that can be produced with mostly agent labor and light human QA.

## Target Reader
Small Austin-area developers, acquisition teams, lenders, and brokers who need fast local signal triage.

## Issue Promise
In under 5 minutes, the reader should understand what changed, why it matters, and what deserves follow-up.

## Fixed Issue Structure

### 1. Executive Summary
- 3-5 bullets on the most decision-relevant changes this period
- one sentence on overall market tone: improving / mixed / deteriorating

### 2. Top Signals This Period
For each signal, capture:
- headline
- source
- date
- submarket
- category: permits / zoning / financing / sales / leasing / supply / demand / policy
- why it matters
- likely impact: positive / neutral / negative

### 3. Submarket Watchlist
Track 3-5 submarkets consistently:
- Central Austin
- East Austin
- Domain / North Austin
- South Austin
- one rotating niche submarket if useful

For each:
- what changed
- what stayed flat
- one risk flag
- one opportunity flag

### 4. Deal-Ops Trigger Feed
Short bullets only:
- new item
- likely implication for acquisitions / underwriting / lender appetite
- urgency: high / medium / low

### 5. This Week's Thesis
One short paragraph:
- what pattern is emerging
- what operators should watch next
- what would invalidate the thesis

### 6. Source Log
Minimum source fields:
- source name
- link or reference
- date checked
- notes on reliability

## Scoring Rubric For Each Signal
- relevance to acquisition decisions: 1-5
- novelty: 1-5
- actionability: 1-5
- confidence in source: 1-5

Only keep items with strong enough scores to justify reader attention.

## Pass / Fail Standard
This template passes validation if:
- one issue can be assembled in under 90 minutes from public sources,
- the output feels decision-useful rather than generic,
- and the format can be repeated without bespoke analyst effort.

## Smallest Next Step
Populate this template once with real Austin public-source items and judge whether the result feels subscription-worthy.
