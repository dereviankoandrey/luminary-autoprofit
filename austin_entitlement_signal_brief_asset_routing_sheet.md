## Default routing by stage

### Stage 1 — first contact
Best asset:
- usually **none**
- just ask permission or test relevance first

### Stage 2 — they say "send it"
Best asset:
- `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_sample_2026-05-15.md`

Why:
- strongest forward-looking proof
- shows named agenda items and operator implications
- best evidence that the product is not generic market commentary

### Stage 3 — they want quick understanding before reading a sample
Best asset:
- `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_landing_page.md`

Why:
- fastest comprehension path
- good for busy operators who will not read a full issue first

### Stage 4 — they question trust / source quality
Best asset:
- `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_process_layer_refresh_2026-05-15.md`

Why:
- tight proof that live public source monitoring is producing concrete forward-looking signals

### Stage 5 — they ask about price or pilot structure
Best asset:
- `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_pilot_offer.md`

Why:
- keeps the commercial ask clean and contained

### Stage 6 — they are interested and want next steps
Best asset:
- `austin_entitlement_signal_brief_pilot_intake_pack.md`

Why:
- converts interest into a structured setup path
