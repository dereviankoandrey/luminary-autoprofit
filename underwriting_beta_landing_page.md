## Core Promise
This is a first-pass underwriting tool for teams that need faster deal triage.

Instead of spending time on every inbound opportunity, use a fixed screening flow to surface:
- recommendation: **GO / REVIEW / NO-GO**
- key metrics
- red flags
- green flags
- concise explanation of why the deal passed or failed the first screen
