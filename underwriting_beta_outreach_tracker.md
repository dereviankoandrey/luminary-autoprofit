# AI Underwriting SaaS — Beta Outreach Tracker

**Last updated:** 2026-04-22 10:00 UTC  
**Purpose:** turn the existing beta target list, scorecard, and beta packet into one lightweight operating tracker for real outreach once Andrey supplies names.

## Why this exists
The business already has:
- a beta target list
- a candidate scorecard
- a beta packet

What was still missing was a single operating page that tracks the outreach sequence from candidate selection through response, demo, feedback, and post-demo decision.

This closes that gap.

## Operating Goal
Move from "we need real beta candidates" to a repeatable first-outreach workflow that can handle 3-5 real names fast once they are provided.

## Recommended Workflow
1. Add real names from Andrey's network
2. Score them using `underwriting_beta_candidate_scorecard.md`
3. Choose the first outreach batch
4. Send or queue the outreach ask
5. Track replies
6. Run demos using `underwriting_beta_packet.md`
7. Record feedback and validation outcome
8. Mark whether each candidate should move toward paid follow-up, continued beta, or no further action

## First Outreach Batch Rules
Use these rules unless Andrey overrides them:
- choose 2 candidates most likely to reply quickly
- choose 2 candidates with the clearest screening pain
- add 1 optional stretch candidate with strong paid potential
- favor warm intros over prestige

## Outreach Status Definitions
- **Not started** — no message prepared or sent yet
- **Queued** — selected for the first batch but not yet sent
- **Sent** — initial outreach sent
- **Replied** — candidate responded
- **Demo scheduled** — time agreed
- **Demo complete** — walkthrough finished
- **Follow-up due** — feedback or next step pending
- **Qualified beta** — good beta fit confirmed
- **Not now** — keep warm but pause
- **Not a fit** — remove from active outreach
