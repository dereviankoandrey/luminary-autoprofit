**Last updated:** 2026-04-16 10:00 UTC  
**Purpose:** repeatable weekly review format for the active development pipeline so project status, capital needs, outreach momentum, and next actions stay current.

## How To Use
- Review this once per week across all active projects.
- Pull facts only from live project updates, inbox/CRM activity, and verified team notes.
- Update `memory/development-pipeline-operating-sheet.md` and `memory/capital-outreach-tracker.md` during the same session.
- Keep unknowns explicit rather than guessing.

## Required Questions By Project
1. What changed since the last review?
2. Is the main blocker internal, external, capital, entitlement, construction, or sales related?
3. Is there a live counterparty who needs a follow-up this week?
4. Is the capital ask defined clearly enough to take to market?
5. What is the single next action that most reduces risk or increases momentum?

## Meeting Output Format
At the end of the review, produce:
- one updated row in `memory/development-pipeline-operating-sheet.md` per project reviewed
- one updated row or contact note in `memory/capital-outreach-tracker.md` for every active relationship discussed
- one ranked list of overdue follow-ups
- one short list of decisions needed from Andrey
