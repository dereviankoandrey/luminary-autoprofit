## Summary of Common Issues Found

### 1. Unverified Statistics (Most Critical)
**Affected:** blog_post_ai_real_estate_development files, linkedin_post_2026-07-01.md, newsletter_section_2026-07-01.md, tweet2_2026-07-01.md, outreach DMs 1 & 4

The "cutting appraisal time by up to/around 70%" claim appears across multiple files without any source citation. Brand Voice Rule #5 requires all data-backed statements to be verifiable.

**Fix:** Either:
- Cite a specific study or industry report as the source
- Remove the percentage and reframe qualitatively (e.g., "significantly faster")
- Use internal validation data if this comes from the underwriting engine testing

### 2. Marketing Filler Language
**Affected:** blog outlines, linkedin_post_2026-07-01.md, newsletter_section_2026-07-01.md, tweet3_2026-07-01.md

Phrases like "future-proof your portfolio," "moving the needle," and "AI-driven insights" appear repeatedly. These are vague claims that don't meet Rule #3 (no vague claims) or Rule #2 (no filler phrases).

**Fix:** Replace with concrete outcomes:
- ❌ "future-proof your portfolio" → ✅ "build a pipeline that adapts to market changes without rework"
- ❌ "moving the needle" → ✅ specify what metric improves and by how much
- ❌ "AI-driven insights" → ✅ name the specific capability (predictive analytics, automated underwriting, etc.)

### 3. Emoji Usage in Social Posts
**Affected:** tweet1_2026-07-01.md, tweet3_2026-07-01.md

Thread posts contain emoji usage ("👇", "🧵") which violates Rule #1 (no emojis or decorative symbols).

**Fix:** Remove all emojis from social media drafts. LinkedIn and Twitter/X threads perform well without them, especially for professional/technical content.
