## Signal 1 (HIGH) — Dog's Head: 2,614-Acre ETJ Development, Annexation, TIRZ, and LGC

**Source:** 5/19/2026 Work Session — Item B003
**Source URL:** https://www.austintexas.gov/council/2026/20260519-wrk
**Agenda PDF:** https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=473411

**What's happening:**
The City Council work session on 5/19 includes a briefing on a **proposed development agreement and future annexation of a 2,614-acre mixed-use development** in the City's ETJ bounded by the Colorado River, US Highway 183, and State Highway 130 — the area generally known as **"Dog's Head."** The briefing includes future creation of a **Tax Increment Reinvestment Zone (TIRZ)** and a **Local Government Corporation (LGC)**.

Presented by Dr. Eric A. Johson, Assistant City Manager.

**Operator implications:**
- A TIRZ for Dog's Head would significantly alter financing mechanics for infrastructure in this corridor, potentially lowering developer cost burdens and accelerating project timelines.
- An LGC structure gives the City and developers a joint governance framework, which changes how future entitlement phases get negotiated.
- 2,614 acres in the ETJ is one of the largest single ETJ development packages on the current docket — any developer, lender, or capital partner with ETJ exposure in this corridor needs to understand the proposed annexation terms.
- This is a work-session briefing, meaning substantive discussion and possible direction to staff will happen before any vote, but it signals strong City intent.

**Watch:**
- Outcome of the 5/19 work session discussion on Item B003.
- Any subsequent regular-meeting item for formal development agreement approval.
- Public comment and neighborhood reaction, which can delay or reshape ETJ annexations.
- TIRZ study scope and timeline if approved at work session.

**Relevance:** Developers with ETJ land positions, lenders structuring entitlement-phase debt, acquisitions teams screening far-to-near ETJ parcels, capital partners tracking Austin mega-project pipeline.
