## Recommended sequencing
1. brain-dump 15-20 warm names in `austin_local_intelligence_bundle_warm_lead_capture_sheet_2026-05-21.md`
2. score the best 10 in `austin_local_intelligence_bundle_first10_selection_sheet_2026-05-21.md`
3. assign each to Cell A, B, or C
4. send in small batches of 2-3
5. log every reply, objection, and product preference
6. pause after 5 tests and review pricing / relevance pattern

## Status definitions
- **queued** — name selected, not sent
- **sent** — first outreach sent
- **sample sent** — proof sent, awaiting reaction
- **qualified** — clear workflow fit confirmed
- **pilot offered** — direct pricing / pilot ask made
- **won** — agreed to pilot
- **not now** — timing issue, possible later follow-up
- **not fit** — poor ICP or wrong workflow

## Cell guide
- **A** — bundle-first direct pilot framing
- **B** — sample-first utility framing
- **C** — brief-versus-bundle learning question
