## Batch Construction Rule
Unless Andrey overrides it, build the first batch as:
- **2 fast-reply candidates**
- **2 strongest pain / workflow-fit candidates**
- **1 optional stretch candidate** with high paid potential

Favor warm access and fast learning over prestige.

## Message Routing Note
Do not send automatically. When ready, outreach should go:
- from **Andrey** directly for the warmest relationships
- from the **closest relationship owner** if Andrey is not the best sender
- by **email, text, or direct message** depending on the strongest existing channel

## Exact Ask Template
Use this unless customized for the relationship.

> I built a lightweight underwriting tool that gives a quick GO / REVIEW / NO-GO on deals before full analysis. I’m looking for a few sharp operators/lenders to give blunt feedback. Would you be open to a 15-minute demo and reaction?

## Reply Handling Rules
### If they say yes
- move to `Demo scheduled`
- use `underwriting_beta_packet.md`
- log outcome in `underwriting_beta_outreach_tracker.md`

### If they express interest but not now
- mark `Not now`
- add follow-up date
- note blocker

### If they do not reply
- wait 5-7 business days
- send one short follow-up
- if still no reply, mark `Keep warm, not now`

## Definition of a Good First Batch
The first batch is good if it produces at least:
- 2 fast replies
- 2 completed demos
- 1 candidate who hits at least 2 validation criteria

## Why this matters
The goal is not just to collect names. The goal is to turn names into a deliberate first learning cycle that produces fast signal on product value and paid potential.
